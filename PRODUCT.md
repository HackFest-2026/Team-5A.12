# CashPilot

CashPilot is a multi-tenant accounts-receivable and cash-flow workspace for Indian businesses. It gives owners, finance teams, customers, and platform operators one shared source of truth for invoices, payments, disputes, approvals, forecasts, and audit history.

## Personas

- CFO / Owner: monitor revenue and cash risk, inspect forecasts, approve financial actions.
- Finance Manager: manage customers, invoices, collections, payments, and reminders.
- Customer: view own invoices and payments, raise disputes, pay outstanding balances.
- Platform Admin: operate tenants, users, access policy, and platform audit records.

## Product promise

Every important number is traceable, every write is permission-checked, and every workflow is exposed through typed APIs so a product-native agent can reason and act without screen scraping.
